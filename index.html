        make-my-own-social / SKILL.md Plain text · works with Claude Code
        Open in Claude

        Opens a new Claude Code session on your computer with the instructions ready — you just press Enter. Needs the Claude desktop app.

        Or download SKILL.md

        How to use it

        1. Download the file.
        2. In Claude Code, put it at ~/.claude/skills/make-my-own-social/SKILL.md — or just paste it into a chat.
        3. Say “make me my own social”. It will ask for your name, your sections and a photo.
